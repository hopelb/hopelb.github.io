Hey! I'm Hope Lumsden-Barry. I'm a freelance designer living and working in Naarm/Melbourne. I make books, zines, websites, brands, exhibitions, and more recently, titles for independent films. You can find an incomplete selection of my work on this site.

I'm also a sessional lecturer at RMIT University. Formerly, I was Experience Design Principal at Paper Giant. A long time ago, I worked at Today. I run a small press called Picolitre Press.
